YouTube Music Skip Solution

Privacy policy

YouTube Music Skip Solution does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable data, browsing history or analytics.

Website addresses

Page URLs are checked locally, on your device, only to detect youtube.com so the extension can keep playback going there. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Payments

All payment transactions are handled by ExtensionPay with Stripe.

Stored on your device only

The extension keeps two small values in your browser’s local extension storage: the install date, and when the “Rate it” link was first shown or used. They never leave your device and are deleted when you remove the extension.

Uninstall page

When you remove the extension, the browser opens skipnomore.site/bye/ with the extension version and browser type (Chrome or Firefox) in the address. If you click a reason there, that reason, version and browser type are counted with the website analytics below. No free text, email or other data is collected.

Permissions

  • scripting
  • youtube.com

Official copy

The official policy text, as linked from the browser stores, is published here: privacy-policy.md.

Website analytics

This website (not the extension) uses Umami, a cookieless analytics tool, to count page views and clicks such as “Add to Chrome”. It sets no cookies, collects no personal data and doesn’t track you across sites; we only see aggregated statistics.
